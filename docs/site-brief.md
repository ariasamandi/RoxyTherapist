# RoxyTherapist — Site Brief

Reference document for building the Roxana therapy practice website.

---

## Overall Vibe

A blend between a **boutique wellness brand** and a **professional healthcare practice**.

| Pillar | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| ☀️ Warm and hopeful | Soft sunrise colors, natural light |
| 🌿 Clean and minimal | Lots of white space |
| 📱 Mobile-friendly | Responsive, touch-friendly layout |
| 📸 Photography | Professional but approachable |
| ✨ Navigation | Find what you need within 2–3 clicks |

---

## Differentiation Strategy

Many therapy websites are very generic:

> "I provide a safe, nonjudgmental space…"

While that's important, almost every therapist says it.

**Instead, focus on what changes for clients:**

- Feel less overwhelmed by anxiety
- Build healthier relationships
- Heal from painful experiences
- Develop confidence in yourself
- Learn practical tools you can use outside of therapy

## Home Page

*The most important page.*

### Hero Section

- **Image:** Large calming image — sunrise, ocean, mountains, soft nature, or a professional photo of Roxana
- **Headline options:**
  - "Helping you find healing, balance, and hope."
  - "Therapy for individuals and couples ready to create meaningful change."
- **CTA button:** Schedule a Free 15-Minute Consultation

### Brief Introduction

- Photo of Roxana
- Opening: "Hi, I'm Roxana…"
- Cover:
  - Passion
  - Approach
  - Warmth
  - Who she helps
- **Not** a resume — keep it personal and inviting

### How I Can Help

Simple icons for:

- ✔ Anxiety
- ✔ Depression
- ✔ Trauma & PTSD
- ✔ Relationship Challenges
- ✔ Self-Esteem
- ✔ Stress & Burnout
- ✔ Life Transitions

### Your Approach

Explain **how therapy feels**, not just modalities.

**Example copy:**

> Therapy isn't just about talking through problems. Together we'll understand what's keeping you stuck, develop practical tools, heal underlying wounds, and create lasting change.

Then mention:

- CBT
- EMDR
- Positive Psychology
- Mindfulness
- Couples Therapy

### Three-Step Process

People love knowing what to expect.

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Step 1** | Schedule a free consultation |
| **Step 2** | Identify your goals and create a personalized plan |
| **Step 3** | Build the skills and confidence to create lasting change |

### Testimonials

- Use if allowed by licensing board and ethics
- Otherwise: anonymous client themes, or remove entirely

### Final CTA

- **Headline:** Ready to begin?
- **Button:** Schedule your free consultation

## Design System

### Colors

Soft palette — avoid harsh black backgrounds.

| Role | Ideas |
|------|-------|
| Base | Cream, warm white |
| Accent | Soft sage, dusty blue, muted terracotta |
| Highlight | Golden sunrise accents |

### Typography

One elegant heading font + one simple body font.

**Headings (choose one):**

- Cormorant Garamond
- Lora
- Playfair Display

**Body (choose one):**

- Inter
- Source Sans
- Lato

### Photography Guidelines

**Avoid:** Generic stock photos of people laughing on a couch.

**Use instead:**

- Sunrise
- Ocean
- Mountains
- Warm windows
- Trees
- Soft natural light
- Roxana's own professional photos

> Authentic imagery feels much more genuine than generic stock.
